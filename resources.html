COURSE GUIDE

The actual datasets, lecture slides, references, and tools behind this course. Use the links in each week’s Agenda to find the right place to start.

COURSE OUTCOMES

What you will get from this course

01

Learn the history of data visualization

Meet the people and landmark works that shaped how we make evidence visible.

02

Learn to see and question data

Notice patterns, omissions, uncertainty, and the choices behind every visual claim.

03

Turn data into visual form

Move from a dataset to exploratory sketches and finished visualizations using analog or digital tools.

04

Communicate an evidence-based idea

Choose visual forms deliberately, explain your decisions, and help an audience understand what matters.

PARTICIPATION EXPECTATIONS

This is a course you join, not watch.

You do not need to arrive as an expert. You do need to be willing to:

01

Express yourself

Bring your own observations, questions, ideas, and visual point of view.

02

Be a team player

Work in groups, help classmates, and take responsibility for shared work.

03

Have dialogue

Ask questions, listen carefully, discuss differences, and be willing to revise.

04

Share and contribute

Show work in progress, offer feedback, and participate—not simply sit and receive information.

GRADING

How your work adds up.

Regular making matters most. The presentations show how those experiments develop into a clear visual argument.
