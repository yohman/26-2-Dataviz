# Week 6 sources and teaching adaptations

The original course deck remains `lectures/w06_lores.pdf`. The replacement uses
the same 960 × 540 canvas, image-led pacing, minimal slide text, and factual
Japanese/English notes in the site viewer.

## Originals

- City and rural population, 1890: https://www.loc.gov/item/2013650430/
  - LOC ppmsca.33873; ink and watercolor, ca. 1900.
  - `assets/images/week06/city-rural.jpg` derives from the LOC `33873u.tif` scan,
    resized proportionally to at most 2200 pixels and JPEG encoded at quality 92.
  - Labels transcribed: 78,139; 8,025; 37,699; 734,952. Unit: people.
  - Population: Black residents of Georgia in the specified settlement categories.
  - Sum: 858,815. Rural share calculated from these four values: about 85.6%.
- Illiteracy: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2013650433/
  - LOC ppmsca.33876; ink and watercolor, ca. 1900.
  - `assets/images/week06/illiteracy.jpg` derives from `33876u.tif` using the same conversion.
  - Labels: 1860 99%; 1870 92%; 1880 81.8%; 1890 67.27%; 1900? 50%?.
  - Retain the final point's uncertainty. The chart alone does not specify an age denominator.
  - The lesson's line chart uses a dashed final segment and open estimated point.
  - Line segments do not represent newly observed intermediate years.
